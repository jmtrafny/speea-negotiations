Keep your work

After the page loads, it makes no network requests. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

Browser storage is tied to this site in this browser and does not follow you to another device. Export JSON is your portable backup. Clearing browser data can erase local saves.

JSON exports contain your complete profile, including private context and archived revisions. Policy-text exports omit selected structured private fields by default, but do not automatically redact your writing.

This erases this app’s browser save and current session. It does not delete files you already exported.

A policy modeler, not a voting guide

Who made this

One person: a SPEEA-represented engineer at Boeing, working on personal time. This page is not a SPEEA product and not a Boeing product. No one at either organization reviewed, endorsed, or sponsored it, and it does not speak for either. SPEEA and Boeing are named only to describe the contract vote it is meant to help with.

What it does

It helps you write down the conditions, priorities, tradeoffs, and evidence rules you want to use before you read a tentative agreement. Decide what matters first, and reading the contract becomes a checklist instead of a scramble. It contains no SPEEA or Boeing contract terms, no bargaining updates, and starts with no weights or salary targets. It does not tell you how to vote.

What the numbers mean

For each topic you keep as a dial, you define a range: a low end (0), a high end (100), and an outcome halfway in value to you (50). Relative “swing” weights describe how much each full improvement matters to you. Weights are not objective importance ratings. Ordering alone cannot supply the size of a tradeoff.

What the checks do not establish

The prototype compares a few of your explicit preferences with a simple additive model. It cannot establish that your preferences are perfectly consistent. Combinations of outcomes may need different rules. Personal conditions and uncertainty still require human interpretation.

Free-text rules are recorded, not executed. The prototype does not parse contracts, validate legal enforceability, map real provisions to scores, forecast bargaining outcomes, or recommend a vote. The optional local sensitivity check changes one weight at a time. It is not exhaustive.

Privacy and sharing

This page uses no external fonts, libraries, analytics, or accounts, and its content security policy blocks it from connecting anywhere after it loads. The host serves the page and sees the request for it, as with any website. Nothing you type is sent to it. Saving in the browser is optional. Files you export and text you copy are not encrypted. The link always opens a blank policy. Share the link rather than a filled-in JSON profile.

“Freeze” is a workflow lock, not a security feature. A revision preserves the prior policy text. Snapshot IDs are non-cryptographic change markers, not signatures. Importing a profile does not certify who created it.
